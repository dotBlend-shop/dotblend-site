About dotBlend

Hello and welcome,

My name is Markus, and I founded dotBlend to make digital products that are genuinely useful and built to last.

dotBlend designs and sells original digital planners and printable templates, delivered as fillable and printable PDF downloads. Every product is our own design, made from scratch for something people need: editable, simple to use and tested carefully before release.

Our first product line is digital dog planners, with Biscuit as their mascot. More lines will follow where there is real demand.

You can find us on Instagram, Threads, Pinterest, TikTok, YouTube and X, in our store at www.dotblend.net, and on Gumroad's marketplace.

For questions or ideas, email us at shop@dotblend.net.

I wish you many productive and mutually rewarding years with us.
