Our posting tools

dotBlend also has a small set of in-house tools, the dotBlend posting tools. They publish dotBlend's own short videos and images to dotBlend's own social media accounts (YouTube, Instagram, Threads, and later Pinterest and TikTok), at the time we choose and only after the founder has approved each post.

The tools are used only by dotBlend's founder, for dotBlend's own accounts. They are not offered to the public, and nobody else can sign in to them. They do not read other people's data, messages or contacts.

Read exactly what the tools access and store in our Privacy Policy, and the rules for this site in our Terms.
