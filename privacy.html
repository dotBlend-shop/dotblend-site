1. Who we are

dotBlend is a small business in Helsinki, Finland, run by its founder, Markus Långstedt. We make digital planners for dog owners and sell them in our store at www.dotblend.net (run by Gumroad). dotBlend is the data controller for the data described here. Contact: shop@dotblend.net.

2. The dotBlend posting tools

The dotBlend posting tools are small programs that run on our own computer. They publish dotBlend's own videos and images to dotBlend's own social media accounts, at a time we choose, and only after the founder has approved each post.

  • Who uses them: only dotBlend's founder, for dotBlend's own accounts. The tools are not offered to the public, and nobody else can sign in to them.
  • What they do not do: they do not read other people's profiles, posts, comments, messages, followers or contacts. They do not collect data about the people who see our posts. They do not use any data for advertising.
  • How sign-in works: the founder signs in on each platform's own login page, in a normal web browser on our computer. The tools never see or store a password. The platform then gives the tools an access token (a kind of key) that only works for the permissions listed below.

3. What each platform connection can do

These are all the permissions (scopes) the tools ask for. Each one is used only to post to, or check, dotBlend's own account.

PlatformPermissionsWhat we use them for
YouTube (Google) youtube.upload, youtube.readonly Upload our own videos and thumbnails to our YouTube channel, and read the channel's name and id to make sure we upload to the right channel.
Instagram (Meta) instagram_basic, instagram_content_publish, pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement, business_management Find the dotBlend Facebook Page and its linked Instagram professional account (@dotblend_shop), read that account's username and id, and publish our own Reels and posts to it.
Threads (Meta) threads_basic, threads_content_publish Read our own Threads username and id, and publish our own posts to our Threads profile.
Pinterest user_accounts:read, boards:read, pins:read, pins:write Read our own account name and our own boards, and create pins (images or videos linking to our shop) on our own boards.
TikTok user.info.basic, video.upload, video.publish Read our own TikTok display name and id, and upload or publish our own videos to our own TikTok account.

A platform connection is only active after the founder has signed in to it. If we stop using a platform, we remove its access (see section 6).

4. How the data is stored and shared

  • Access tokens are stored only on our own Windows computer, in the Windows Credential Manager (an encrypted password store). They are never written into log files, put on a website or sent to anyone else.
  • A simple log on the same computer records what was posted: the post's id or link, the platform and the time. It contains nothing about other people.
  • The content we post (our own videos, images and captions) is sent only to the platform it is meant for. To post on Threads, the tools use the public link of the same video already published on our Instagram account.
  • We do not sell, rent or share data from these platforms with anyone, and we do not use it for advertising, profiling or training AI models.
  • How long we keep it: tokens are kept until they expire or until we sign out or remove access. Logs are kept as our own record of what we published.

5. Google API Services (YouTube)

dotBlend's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Uploads to YouTube are also covered by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Removing access and deleting data

Access can be removed at any time on each platform. When access is removed, the stored token stops working.

  • Google / YouTube: myaccount.google.com/permissions
  • Facebook and Instagram: Facebook → Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations (or Apps and websites), and in Instagram → Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites
  • Threads: Threads → Settings → Account → Website permissions
  • Pinterest: Settings → Security and logins → Apps (pinterest.com/settings/security)
  • TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions

Data deletion requests: to ask us to delete any data our tools hold about an account, email shop@dotblend.net with the subject "Data deletion request" and the account name. We delete the token and any log lines about that account within 30 days and reply to confirm. Because the tools only connect to dotBlend's own accounts, in practice they hold no data about anyone else.

7. This website

dotblend.net is a plain website with no sign-in, no forms, no cookies, no analytics and no advertising trackers. It is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may record technical data such as visitors' IP addresses for security and to keep the service running; see the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

9. Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you can ask to see, correct or delete personal data we hold about you, or object to how we use it. Email shop@dotblend.net and we will answer within one month. You can also complain to the Finnish Data Protection Ombudsman (tietosuoja.fi).

Our website, shop and tools are not aimed at children under 13.
